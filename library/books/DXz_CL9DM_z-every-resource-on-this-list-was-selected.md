The Impact of the Highly Improbable

Nassim Nicholas Taleb

Taleb’s most widely read work introduces the concept that would
reshape how a generation of risk managers and investors think
about tail events: the Black Swan a highly improbable event with
massive consequences that, in retrospect, appears inevitable. His
central argument is that standard statistical tools, including
variance and standard deviation, are built on assumptions of
normal distribution that systematically underestimate the frequency
and magnitude of extreme events what statisticians call fat tails.
