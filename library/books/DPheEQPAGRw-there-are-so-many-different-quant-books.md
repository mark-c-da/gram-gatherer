If you want to break into quant, here are the books you need to read. Hi, I'm Julia. I teach you about options theory and elbow trading. Stay till the end to hear my number one pick. First is systematic trading by Robert Carver. It gives you the intellectual foundation to build, test, and manage trading systems like a professional. It's less about finding that holy grail strategy and more about building a robust system that survives the real world. My next pick is Bayesian Statistics the Funway by Will Kurt. This is a great way to prep for that probability and brain teaser round of a quant interview. Thinking strategically is my next pick. While it's not a book about trading per se, it does talk about the mechanics of game theory and how they can be applied to help you think like a quant and also give you a competitive edge in life. Then we have my favorite option volatility and pricing by Sheldon Naitenberg. This is the holy grail of quant books. This is typically the first book that's given to people just starting out in the industry and it's one of the most comprehensive guides to trading strategies and options theory out there. Share this with a friend looking to break in a quant and fall for more.
