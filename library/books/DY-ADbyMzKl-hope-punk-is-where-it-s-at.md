If you love the positivity of Project Hill Mary, here are some wholesome sci-fi books you should read. If watching or reading Project Hill Mary made you fall in love with Earth and humanity, I would highly recommend this piece of literary fiction that won the Booker Prize. Follows a group of astronauts as they orbit in a satellite around the Earth. Orbital by Samantha Harvey focuses a lot on this feeling of awe that you get when you look down at the world from up above. It's gorgeously written and will give you a newfound appreciation of this little rock that we are all on. But if you want something that focuses a little bit more on the actual characters, I have another book that I would recommend that's also about a group of astronauts that are in space but are going to research a bunch of planets, but actually focuses more on the interactions between the astronauts and how they keep each other going as they are away from their home and their families for so long. That is to be taught a fortunate by Becky Chambers. What I also love is that this is a sci-fi that actually questions the idea of going to other planets and disrupting the local ecosystem. Becky Chambers in general is just a queen of wholesome sci-fi. I mean, you know I love the monk and robot series. If you fell in love with the friendship between Rocky and Ryland, you're going to love the friendship between the team monk and the robot in this series. It's wholesome sci-fi because it explores connection between humans through a utopian futuristic world. And if you love the humor in Project Hill Mary, you're going to love all systems read by Martha Well. This is about like a guard cyborg who overrides his own like internal system, becomes self-aware and starts giving very funny commentary on the things around him and he realizes that he actually prefers using his internal technological system to, you know, watch TV or something. It's also a bit more action-packed than the other books that I recommended so far. So if you liked that about Project Hill Mary, you should read all systems read. But I mostly am looking for more wholesome sci-fi books like this because I completely fell in love with it. So if you have any recommendations or books that you would want me to read, please let me know.
