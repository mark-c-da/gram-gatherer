# Six niche nonfiction books to read along

**Author:** Nathan Shuherk
**Why save:** Reel recs — six lesser-known nonfiction books he is about to read.

- *The Revolution Will Not Be Funded* — how basic needs got handed to nonprofits, and how those nonprofits sit inside capitalism
- *Heaven Is a Place on Earth* — a trip across U.S. communes and intentional communities looking for working anarchism
- *Against Technoableism* — disability, tech, and why treating tech as a “cure” is ableist
- *Wronged and Dangerous* — manliness influencers and the harm in the masculinity they sell
- *Subprime Attention Crisis* — advertising as the internet’s shaky foundation
- *Tree Thieves* — the black market in lumber as a billion-dollar true-crime story

six nonfiction books that you've probably never seen before that I'm going to be reading soon and that you might want to pick up and read along with me. The revolution will not be funded is about how we've created society where basic needs are met by nonprofits instead of the government and how nonprofits are part of the capitalist hellscape we're living in. Heaven is a place on earth as a personal journey across the United States looking at communes and intentional communities trying to find true functional anarchism. This techno-ableism is about the blend of disability and technology and how perceiving these things as a cure is really ableist. Wronged and dangerous is about the growth of manliness influencers, how the ideas of masculinity being petaled are toxic and harmful for everyone. Sub-prime attention crisis is about how advertising runs the internet and if that system were to fail, everything would fail and it's bound to happen. Tree thieves is an investigation into the black markets of lumber, a billion dollar true crime story that basically no one's talking about.
