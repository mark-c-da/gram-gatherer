# It's Not Exercise; It's Just Moving Around

**Coach:** John Noel | Health & Fitness Coach
**Why save:** Home conditioning — hopping, plank, and core, 3 rounds each with 60s rest. Caption had the full workout.

## Hopping conditioning

3 rounds
- 20 jumping jacks
- 20 twist hops
- 10 floor to ceiling taps
- 60 seconds rest

## Plank conditioning

3 rounds
- 20 plank knee taps
- 20 plank arm and leg extensions
- 10 pushups
- 60 seconds rest

## Core conditioning

3 rounds
- 20 bicycle kicks
- 20 heel taps
- 10 lounge chair knee tucks
- 60 seconds rest
