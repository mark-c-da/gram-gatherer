# Six new albums too good to sleep on

**Artist:** Don Chenz
**Notes:** Weekly recs reel — six new hip-hop / neo-soul albums. Caption named the pitch; titles came from the transcript.

- *The Sword and the Soaring* — Navy Blue. Producer-songwriter growth; sword = struggle, soaring = freedom. Favorites: Sunlight of the Spira; 24 Gospel (feat. Real Sweatcher).
- *Inside Out* — Flower Child (Atlanta). Color-theory neo-soul with a wide collaborator cast. Favorites: Bittersweet (with Malaya); Reasons Why (with James Tillman).
- *Mood Control* — Black Odyssey. Psychedelic, genre-bending; framed as a hijacked radio broadcast. Favorites: Waves; Heartbreak.
- *Hotep Luxury* — Maroof (New Jersey). Neo-soul / jazz / hip-hop; features including SZA, Mick Jenkins, Fousheé, Vic Mensa. Favorites: PT Cruiser (with SZA); Light My Fire (feat. Jesse Boykins III).
- *Scram* — Domo Genesis & Grey Matter. Domo’s pen over Grey Matter’s sample-driven soul beats. Favorites: Clocking to You (feat. Evidence and Kurt Rambus).
- *2002* — Shy Guy & Jay Boogs. Golden-era soul, modern execution. Favorites: Dolasign (feat. Lord Sko); Belly of the Beast (feat. Life of Tom). Jay Boogs also had *We All Need Help* with Larisi last year.

## Raw

These 6 new albums are INCREDIBLE and way too good for you to be sleeping on #hiphop #rap #rnb

Here's six albums that drop this week that are too good for you to be sleeping on, starting with The Sword and The Soaring by Navy Blue. Navy Blue continues to grow as both the producer and a songwriter and his latest offering is his most focused body of work yet. And the album's title is the perfect metaphor for its subject matter as The Sword represents struggle and working through hardships and The Soaring represents freedom and transcendence. My favorite songs are Sunlight of the Spira and 24 Gospel Featuring Real Sweatcher. Inside out by Flower Child, the Atlanta-based artist and producer crafted a lush soundscape using color theory as the album's framework. And he invites a wide-ranging cast of collaborators who together create a Neo Soul masterpiece. My favorite songs are Bittersweet with Malaya and Reasons Why with James Tillman. Mood Control by Black Odyssey. Black Odyssey is truly in a lane of his own with this psychedelic genre-bending album that's an immersive experience as the listener is tuned into a hijacked radio broadcast before the FCC cuts it off. My favorite tracks are Waves and Heartbreak. Mouladek, Hotep Luxury by Maroof. The New Jersey-based artist blends Neo Soul Jazz and Hip Hop for an album that's equal part style and substance. And there are excellent features ranging from SZA to Mick Jenkins to Faushay to Vic Mensa. My favorite songs are PT Cruiser with SZA and Light My Fire Featuring Jesse Boykins III. Scram by Domo Genesis and Grey Matter. Domo Genesis has evolved from his odd future days and continues to sharpen his pen and refine his skills as a lyricist. And producer Grey Matter provides the perfect backdrop of soulful, sample-driven beats for Domo to showcase his skills. My favorite tracks are Clocking to You featuring Evidence and Kurt Rambus. Finally, 2002 by Shy Guy and Jay Boogs. This project has that golden era soul but is executed to feel fresh and modern for today's Hip Hop landscape. I love the production from Jay Boogs who last year released a collab, album with Larisi titled We All Need Help, and rapper Shy Guy slides on these beats with the help of some of my favorite rising MCs like Lord Scow, Life of Tom and Black Sam. My favorites are Dolasign featuring Lord Scow and Belly of the Beast featuring Life of Tom. Let me know in the comments what other new releases are being slept on and make sure you check out my music discovery playlist called The Chens List For More.
